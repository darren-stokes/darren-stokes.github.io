About Me

I lead platform teams that make engineering faster, safer and calmer.

I'm the Platform Team Lead at Ilkari in Málaga, where my team owns Kubernetes, CI/CD and DevSecOps for the company's domains product. I stepped into the role in September 2025 and was formally appointed in June 2026.

Behind that sit 14+ years in infrastructure, DevOps and platform engineering, from a global trading firm's datacentres in Dublin to cloud-native platforms in Spain. I still know the stack well enough to make good technical calls and to earn the trust of the engineers I lead.

I care about building teams where engineers have clear ownership, room to grow, and the platform support to ship with confidence. I hold an MSc in Management of Information Systems from Trinity College Dublin, and I work in English and Spanish.
